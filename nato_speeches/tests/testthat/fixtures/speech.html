Minister's speech at the NATO summit

Speech by the Minister of Defence, The Hague, 24 June 2025.

Secretary General, colleagues, ladies and gentlemen. It is an honour to address this Alliance at a moment when our collective defence is tested as never before.

We have committed to investing five percent of our gross domestic product in defence and security. This is not a number on paper; it is a promise to our citizens and to each other.

An attack on one is an attack on all.
